\section{Comparison}
\label{sec:datasets-comparison}

The two datasets share split sizes and a common visual-rendering
scheme but differ markedly in graph structure and inference
tractability. Table~\ref{tab:datasets-comparison} summarises the
relevant aspects side by side.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{tabular}{l l l}
\hline
\textbf{Aspect} & \textbf{HMC} & \textbf{Sudoku} \\
\hline
Task type                 & sequence labeling
                          & structured prediction \\
Graph                     & linear chain
                          & row/column/box constraint graph \\
$|V|$                     & $T = 30$
                          & $81$ \\
$|E|$                     & $29$
                          & $810$ \\
Label set $K$             & $10$
                          & $9$ \\
Total examples            & $50{,}000$
                          & $50{,}000$ \\
Train / val / test        & $30{,}000\,/\,10{,}000\,/\,10{,}000$
                          & $30{,}000\,/\,10{,}000\,/\,10{,}000$ \\
Symbolic input shape      & $T \times K$
                          & $81 \times 9$ \\
Visual input shape        & $T \times 1 \times 28 \times 28$
                          & $81 \times 1 \times 28 \times 28$ \\
MAP inference             & exact (Viterbi, $O(T K^{2})$)
                          & NP-hard (approximate; Chapter~\ref{chap:method}) \\
Source                    & synthetic
                          & Kaggle \texttt{bryanpark/sudoku} \\
Role in this thesis       & tightness validation
                          & main task \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption[Datasets summary]{Side-by-side summary of the HMC and
Sudoku datasets.}
\label{tab:datasets-comparison}
\end{table}

Both datasets are built and loaded through the same
\texttt{build\_*\_benchmark} / \texttt{load\_*\_benchmark} API in
\texttt{mnlearn.data}. The two main differences highlighted in the
table are the graph structure (chain vs constraint graph) and the
consequent tractability of MAP inference (exact on HMC, NP-hard on
Sudoku).
