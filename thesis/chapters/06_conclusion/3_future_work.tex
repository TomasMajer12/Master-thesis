\section{Future work}
\label{sec:conclusion-future}

Several directions extend the present work. \emph{Exact
decoding} at evaluation time --- via an integer program over
the constraint graph --- would isolate the Belief Propagation
gap discussed in
Section~\ref{subsec:discussion-failure-modes}; the present BP
decoder is fast enough for inner-loop training but its
approximation quality on the Sudoku graph is not characterised.
\emph{Scaling} the training-set size into the hundreds of
thousands (the Kaggle source has $\sim 10^{6}$ puzzles
available) would test whether the present learning curve
flattens or continues to improve. Other \emph{graph families}
--- text labelling CRFs, image segmentation --- would test the
framework outside the Sudoku-specific all-different setting.
